作品的源资产为 `works/metro-rush/assets/preview.webp` 与 `works/strike-arena/assets/preview.webp`；启动台使用相同内容的 `assets/metro-rush-preview.webp` 与 `assets/strike-arena-preview.webp`。首页卡片不额外放大或平移这两张封面。

## Metro Rush 最终提示词

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: landscape cover artwork for an original browser 3D runner game named Metro Rush. This is standalone artwork, NOT a UI mockup.
Primary request: create a polished, cohesive editorial game-cover illustration of a runner weaving through an urban metro railway at sunset. Original character and setting, no recognizable commercial IP.
Scene: three straight parallel railway lanes converge to ONE coherent vanishing point in a believable sunlit city corridor; grounded commuter trains on the outer lanes, small coin pickups tracing a visible route, station gantries, restrained city architecture. A clearly readable runner seen from behind in the foreground on the middle lane, sporty jacket, cap, backpack, mid-stride running pose.
Style: premium stylized 3D game key art, refined geometry and materials, strong silhouette, art-directed and elegant; sophisticated warm sunlight with slate-gray tracks and softly muted city tones. Crisp detail, atmospheric depth, no neon blue-purple or acid-green glow.
Composition/framing: WIDE landscape 16:9. Main runner fully visible, central composition. All important subjects safely inside the central 70 percent of the width and central 75 percent of height so a centered 16:10 crop retains them. Trains must share rail perspective and be physically planted on tracks, no tilted or floating train. Bottom 15 percent is simple foreground tracks, safe for a small HTML caption overlay. Strong image at thumbnail size, no extraneous collage frames.
Constraints: No text of any kind, no letters, no title, no game logo, no watermark, no UI, no buttons, no HUD, no typography, no frame. Natural correct anatomy, consistent rail/trains perspective, no disconnected limbs. Create a complete original landscape illustration.
```

No text, no letters, no logo, no watermark, no UI, no buttons, no HUD, no crosshair, no score, no typography, no borders.
